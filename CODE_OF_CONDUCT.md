# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this educational project respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, education, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep sample data fictional and protect personal information.
- Credit sources and contributors honestly.
- Accept corrections and help learners understand the reasoning.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information,
plagiarism, or deliberately misleading project claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact
options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove
content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and is tailored to
this small educational repository.
